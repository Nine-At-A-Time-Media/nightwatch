# Stories: alerting

Roles: **team member**, **on-call**, **reporter**.

- As a team member, I am told when a new issue first appears.
- As a team member, I am told when a resolved issue comes back.
- As a team member, I am told when an issue happens more than some number
  of times in some window, or to more than some number of people.
- As a team member, I can narrow an alert to an environment, a level, a
  tag, a release, or the issue's owner.
- As a team member, I get one email that collects a burst of alerts,
  instead of one email for each.
- As a team member, I am not alerted twice about the same issue within a
  quiet period I choose.
- As an on-call, I am paged when the error rate of a project crosses a
  threshold, and told when it recovers.
- As a team member, I can reply to an alert email, and my reply becomes a
  comment on the issue.
- As a team member, I can follow the issues I touched, and hear about
  them, and stop hearing about the rest.
- As a team member, I can send alerts to a webhook, and later to chat.
- As a reporter, I am told about my report, and never about anything
  else.
